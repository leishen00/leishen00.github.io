This study systematically explored the role of complement and coagulation cascade (CCC) genes in lower-grade glioma (LGG) through integrated bioinformatics analysis. The results indicate that CCC genes are closely associated with patient prognosis, immune microenvironment, and potential response to chemotherapy, providing insights for immunotherapy and novel chemotherapeutic strategies in LGG.
