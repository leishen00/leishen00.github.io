This study introduces a novel “bridge-layered” decompression technique for treating vertebral artery–involved hemifacial spasm (VA-involved HFS). The “bridge” component lifts the tortuous vertebral artery trunk to relieve compression on the root exit zone (REZ) of the facial nerve, while the “layered” component isolates and decompresses branch vessels to achieve full decompression of all responsible vessels. Clinical results from 62 patients showed high efficacy, with immediate or delayed cure in almost all cases, no recurrence during follow-up, and minimal complications, demonstrating that the “bridge-layered” technique is a safe and effective approach for VA-involved HFS with potential for broader clinical application.
