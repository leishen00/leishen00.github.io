This case expands the spectrum of clinical manifestations of anti-NMDAR encephalitis, highlighting that motor aphasia can be an initial symptom in adolescent patients. We report an adolescent patient who presented with motor aphasia as the first symptom, indicating that anti-NMDAR encephalitis should be considered in adolescents with sudden-onset motor aphasia, in addition to adults. Moreover, the peripheral nerve axonal damage observed in this patient warrants further investigation.
