This study explored the impact of obesity on surgical outcomes in children with refractory epilepsy. Findings showed that obese children experienced higher intraoperative blood loss compared with nonobese children, while obesity was not associated with operation time or postoperative fever. These results underscore the importance of early weight management in pediatric epilepsy patients.
